08 — Thermodynamics

ΔG = ΔH − T·ΔS · q = m·c·ΔT

Gibbs free energy tells you whether a reaction is spontaneous at a given temperature; the heat equation tells you how much energy a mass change of temperature absorbs or releases.

Formula used ΔG = ΔH − T·ΔS · q = m·c·ΔT
spontaneity + energy
① Gibbs free energy — ΔG = ΔH − T·ΔS
② Heat exchange — q = m·c·ΔT
Presets
How this works
ΔG = ΔH − T·ΔS decides spontaneity: negative ΔG is spontaneous, positive is not, zero is equilibrium. When ΔH and ΔS share a sign, T* = ΔH/ΔS is the crossover where the reaction flips. The heat equation q = m·c·ΔT reports energy in joules — positive q is absorbed (endothermic), negative is released (exothermic).
