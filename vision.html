PRO

Point the camera. Find the element.

Take a photo of an object and Atomurus marks, with a green dot, where each chemical element probably is, with the confidence of the estimate. Every hit or miss you confirm trains the precision of the next analyses.

Frame the object and tap Shoot

How it works

  1. The photo is downscaled on your device and sent for analysis.
  2. The model points out 1 to 12 regions with the dominant element of the material and a confidence.
  3. The confidence shown is adjusted by the accuracy history of that element, confirmed by users.
  4. When you mark ✓ or ✗ (and say which it was), the correction joins the memory used in the next analyses.

A photo cannot prove chemical composition: the result is an estimate from appearance (colour, lustre, texture, context). Alloys and coatings can mislead. Photos are not stored; only the result and your feedback.
