The Elements,
Examined.

Atomurus is a reading room for matter — an interactive periodic table, atomic models, and molecular studies, composed with the care of a scientific journal.

Since antiquity the search for the fundamental constituents of nature has been written down, catalogued, and revised. Atomurus continues that tradition in a living document — one you can open, turn, and explore at the resolution of a single electron.

Here the periodic table is not a widget but a printed plate; trends are not charts but arguments; and every isotope carries the weight of its own quiet history.

Layout philosophy

  • Editorial grid with hard ink rules and baseline rhythm
  • Asymmetric two-column hero, like a journal opener
  • Figures, captions & footnotes carry meaning
  • Generous margins; reading over scanning

Periodic integration

  • Table as an engraved "specimen plate"
  • Hover draws the element onto Fig. 01
  • Watercolour category washes, not blocks

Motion

  • Restrained — pages settle, ink doesn't bounce
  • Underlines draw on hover; cells lift like paper
  • Page-turn fades between sections

Signature details

  • Drop caps & running issue number
  • "Fig." labels & corner registration marks
  • Wax-seal monogram colophon
