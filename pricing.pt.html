§ 02 Free vs Pro

  • SEO engine stays free. Table, elements and articles remain open — Pro sells productivity, not the basics.
  • Pro removes ads for paid, trial and admin sessions via server-side entitlement.
  • Trial is automatic: create an account and the first 30 days run as Pro.

§ 03 What we will not lock

The main periodic table, element encyclopedia pages and Explore articles stay free. Atomurus Pro should feel like a better way to study — not a wall in front of chemistry.
