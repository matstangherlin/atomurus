Dilution

Exercises

  1. A 2 mol/L solution, 10 mL of it, is diluted with water down to 0.5 mol/L. What is the final volume? (basic)

    Answer

    V₂ = 40 mL

    Worked solution
    1. Diluting does not change the amount of solute, so C₁V₁ = C₂V₂. The data: C₁ = 2 mol/L · V₁ = 10 mL · C₂ = 0.5 mol/L
    2. Isolate V₂. V₂ = C₁V₁ / C₂
    3. Substitute (both volumes in the same unit). V₂ = (2 mol/L × 10 mL) / 0.5 mol/L = 40 mL
