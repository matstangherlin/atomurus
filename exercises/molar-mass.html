Exercises

  1. Calculate the molar mass of H₂O. (basic)

    Answer

    M = 18.015 g/mol

    Worked solution
    1. Count the atoms of each element in the formula. H: 2 · O: 1
    2. Look up each atomic mass in g/mol (abridged standard atomic weights, CIAAW). A(H) = 1.008 · A(O) = 15.999
    3. Multiply each atomic mass by its number of atoms. 2 × 1.008 = 2.016 · 1 × 15.999 = 15.999
    4. Add the contributions: the sum is the molar mass. M = 2.016 + 15.999 = 18.015 g/mol

    If you got 17.007 g/mol, you added each element only once. The subscript gives the number of atoms: multiply the atomic mass by it.
