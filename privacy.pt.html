Atomurus does not require an account, an email or any personal registration to use any feature. We do not run a user database, and we do not collect or store personally identifiable information on our servers.

§ 01 Who we are

Atomurus ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at atomurus.com. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you visit Atomurus, by whom, and what choices you have.

§ 02 Information we do not collect

Because Atomurus is open and account-free, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Passwords or login credentials (there is no login)
  • Payment information (the site is free)

§ 03 Information stored locally in your browser

To remember your preferences across visits, Atomurus stores a few values in your browser's localStorage. These values never leave your device and are not sent to us:

  • Theme — whether you prefer light or dark mode
  • Language — your selected interface language
  • Display preferences — element card size, font size, lanthanide/actinide visibility, atomic mass toggle, animations on/off, heatmap mode

You can clear these at any time through your browser's site-data settings.

§ 04 Analytics — Google Analytics 4

Atomurus uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-Q3ME3FMB8X) to understand aggregated, anonymous usage — for example, which pages are visited most often and roughly which countries our visitors come from. Google Analytics may set cookies and collect:

  • Truncated/anonymized IP address
  • Device and browser type
  • Approximate geographic location (country/region level)
  • Pages viewed and time on site

We do not link this data to any individual identity. You can opt out of Google Analytics globally by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

§ 05 Advertising — Google AdSense

Atomurus displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its third-party vendors may use cookies and identifiers to:

  • Serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraudulent or repeated impressions

You can review and control how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads or via the Digital Advertising Alliance.

§ 06 Anti-spam — Google reCAPTCHA v3

Atomurus uses Google reCAPTCHA v3 to protect the site from automated abuse. reCAPTCHA runs invisibly in the background and collects hardware and software information (such as device and application data) and sends it to Google for analysis. Use of reCAPTCHA is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

§ 07 Cookies

The cookies set on Atomurus come from third parties (Google Analytics, Google AdSense, Google reCAPTCHA). We do not set first-party cookies for tracking. By using the site you consent to the use of these third-party cookies; you can disable cookies in your browser at any time, though some advertising or anti-spam features may behave differently.

§ 08 Children's privacy

Atomurus is intended for a general audience including secondary-school students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since the site requires no registration, no personal data is submitted to us in any case. If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns, please consult our Terms of Use.

§ 09 Third-party links

Atomurus may link to third-party websites (for example, scientific references, library projects or sources). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites — please review their own policies.

§ 10 Data security

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, the security exposure of Atomurus is intentionally minimal. The site is served over HTTPS.

§ 11 Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, the GDPR in the European Union or the LGPD in Brazil), you may have rights regarding personal data processed about you by third-party services such as Google. To exercise those rights, please refer directly to Google's own privacy controls linked above.

§ 12 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" at the top of this page.

§ 13 Contact

For privacy-related questions, write to contato@atomurus.com or use the Contact form. Messages sent through the form are delivered to our inbox by Mailgun (our email provider) — we do not store contact details in any database.
