Bug reports
Use the subject "Report a bug" and describe step-by-step. Include browser, operating system and the affected element/page if applicable.

Press, partnership and educational licensing requests are welcome. Mention your institution and intended use case.

Atomurus now supports account creation and login for the private workspace. Use /login to create an account, sign in and keep your study access linked to your email.
