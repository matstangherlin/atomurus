## Full description (max 4000)
Atomurus is a chemistry app for understanding, not just memorizing. Periodic table, virtual lab, isomerism, 3D molecules and step-by-step calculators, all in one place.

Free, no sign-up, no ads, and it works offline.

THE FULL PERIODIC TABLE
• All 118 elements, with data from reference sources such as IUPAC, NIST and CODATA
• Colour the table by category, electronegativity, atomic mass, physical state or each element's real colour
• A page for every element: electron configuration, density, melting and boiling points, isotopes, uses and discovery history
• Periodic trends in charts

VIRTUAL CHEMISTRY LAB
• Build your bench with more than 30 pieces: beakers, flasks, burettes, condensers, Bunsen burners and more
• Type what you want to make, like "0.5 mol/L NaCl solution in 100 mL", and the app works out the mass and prepares the solution
• Follow volume, mass, temperature and pH in real time
• Watch reactions happen with their chemical equation: iron displacing copper, acid and baking soda fizzing, indicators changing colour
• Step-by-step guided experiments such as distillation, fermentation, sugar refining and reflux, with the reason behind each step

ISOMERISM
• Type any molecular formula and see every constitutional isomer drawn
• The IUPAC name of each isomer, its functional group and the type of isomerism between two compounds (chain, position, functional group, metamerism, tautomerism)
• Chiral carbons, cis/trans isomerism and the number of stereoisomers, with a 3D model

MOLECULES AND ATOMS IN 3D
• Molecules in 3D with geometry, polarity and a structural diagram
• Build any organic molecule from its formula
• Dalton, Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr and quantum atomic models for any element
• Allotropes of carbon, oxygen, sulfur and phosphorus

STEP-BY-STEP CALCULATORS
• Molar mass with percentage composition, including hydrates
• pH and pOH, dilution (C₁V₁ = C₂V₂), ideal gas (PV = nRT), unit converter and scientific calculator
• Significant figures and units at every step

ARTICLES
• Articles on the mole, acids and bases, chemical reactions, states of matter, isotopes and major accidents in the history of chemistry

MADE FOR STUDENTS
Great for high school, exam preparation and first-year university chemistry. In English and Portuguese, with light and dark themes.

PRIVACY
The app does not collect or share personal data. What you do stays on your device.
Privacy policy: https://atomurus.com/app-privacy

The virtual lab is an educational simulation. Do not reproduce experiments outside a proper laboratory and without supervision.
